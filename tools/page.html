Setup

Two effects raise the same score

We LoRA fine-tune LLaVA-1.5 on 1,000 NoCaps validation images, each with one of its ten reference captions, repeated d times inside a fixed 130,000-example training mix. Half of NoCaps is never trained on. Then we ask two separate questions.

Style adaptation

The model starts writing in NoCaps' short, one-sentence register on every image, including images it never saw. Measured on unseen images: caption length, boilerplate.

Reference memorization

The model reproduces the specific training caption of an exposed image. Measured on seen images: exact match, longest shared n-gram, and ΔRMI (how much more likely the training caption becomes than the nine held-out ones).
